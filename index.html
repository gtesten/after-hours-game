YOUR SPACE

Settings & privacy.

The game works without accounts, subscriptions, or a backend.

Play safely

For consenting adults aged 18 and over. Ask before physical contact, honor everyone's boundaries and skip decisions, and pause or stop whenever needed. You may change a preference at any time.

Private by design

Player names, preferences, challenges, and game progress are saved in this browser's local storage. Nothing is uploaded to the game creator. If you use another browser or device, it won't automatically receive your data.

Warning: backup files may contain players' preferences and personal challenges. Keep them private.

Install on your phone

iPhone: Open the published website in Safari, choose Share, then Add to Home Screen. Mac/PC: Open the same link in any modern browser; Chrome or Edge can install it as an app from the browser menu.

Once visited, the game may work offline thanks to its cached assets. For the most reliable first use, load it online once.

About After Hours

An original, free, private party game. No advertising, tracking scripts, accounts, or paid features. The built-in challenge deck is playful but non-graphic; you can write and import private custom cards in the editor.
