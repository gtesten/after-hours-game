Quality control

We enforce required fields and supported categories, reject unsupported placeholders and scope types, and compare against existing custom cards.

Expected format: a JSON object with a challenges array, a JSON array, or CSV headers id,type,level,scope,text,position,art,seconds,points.

Advanced categories receive a human-review warning rather than automatic AI approval. A warning cannot be bulk-approved.
